# Mira — Next Product Plan

## Immediate objective
Build a complete vertical slice of two connected chapters that proves Mira can sustain an endless, emotionally coherent mystery.

### Chapter 1 — Percy’s Choice
**Question:** Why did Percy ignore recall?

### Chapter 2 — The Passenger Signal
**Question:** What came home inside Percy?

## Priorities
1. Make the first five minutes exceptional: concrete contradictions, natural combination motivation, restrained Mira responses, and a clear realization that Percy deliberately protected something alive.
2. Add competing beliefs with consequences. Player-selected context should affect which explanation Mira favors, what she asks next, which memories become relevant, and how confident or defensive she becomes.
3. Fully develop The Passenger Signal rather than adding many shallow branches: transmission through Percy → familiar rhythms → possible reconstruction of Sayegh → memory vs imitation vs contact → altered mission record.
4. Make local Gemma consequential after GPU verification: remember unusual combinations, reference prior reasoning, offer constrained alternatives, adapt to accumulated guilt/trust, and avoid repetition.
5. Add persistent relationship development without visible meters: trust in player/Control, willingness to self-question, attachment to Percy, fear of sample, acceptance of uncertainty.
6. Create a session/share artifact appropriate to the fiction: a concise truth, distinctive exchange, named theory, or shareable conversation excerpt—not a generic score.

## Product constraints
- Chat-first; conversation is the game.
- No dashboard, case-file UI, diorama, score-driven arcade layer, or procedural noun spam.
- Every combination must have a concrete, intelligible consequence; only evidence-backed discoveries become fragments.
- Major truths permanently change Mira and expose a deeper mystery.
- Authored engine controls canonical facts; local AI provides contextual interpretation and continuity.
- Mobile-first and fully persona-playtested.

## Definition of success
- A new player can summarize Chapter 1: “Percy wasn’t lost. He disobeyed Mira to protect something alive—and Mira had erased why.”
- Player choices can produce plausible but incomplete theories, not merely reveal recipes in different orders.
- Chapter 2 proves that a resolution can open a genuinely different mystery.
- Mira’s relationship and language evolve across sessions.
- Local AI, when available, is recognizable through memory and adaptation rather than technical labels.
